Watershed Delineation & River Trace Tool

An automated watershed analysis tool that works with a free global open-source DEM - making it instantly usable anywhere in the world - as well as custom DEM uploads for higher resolution or localized terrain data. Select a river or pour point and it automatically delineates watershed boundaries, traces downstream flow paths, and maps the full drainage network. Built for water resource management, flood risk assessment, and hydrological research without requiring advanced GIS software.
